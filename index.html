The Lore

Why do we go to war with colors? The ancient files have the answers.

Ignition: Good vs Evil

Before the colors, comes the fire. Holika Dahan commemorates the ultimate victory of devotion over arrogance. The demon king Hiranyakashipu tried to burn his own son, Prahlad, with the help of his fire-immune sister, Holika.

But the universe had other plans. Prahlad's devotion to Lord Vishnu shielded him, while Holika's immunity was revoked. Today, we burn the pyre to incinerate negativity and reset the system for a colorful tomorrow.

Saturation: Divine Love

The chaotic splashing of colors? That's a direct patch from Lord Krishna. Legend has it, the blue-skinned deity was playfully envious of Radha's fair complexion.

His mother Yashoda told him to just paint her face whatever color he wanted. He took it literally! This playful prank in Vrindavan evolved into a nationwide festival. It breaks down social firewalls, equalizing everyone under a thick layer of neon gulal.
